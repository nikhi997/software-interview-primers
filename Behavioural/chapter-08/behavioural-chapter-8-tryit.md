# Chapter 8: The questions you ask — Try it

*Answers for the Try it questions in [behavioural-chapter-8.md](behavioural-chapter-8.md).*

1. Write five questions you'd genuinely want answered about a team you're interviewing with — and check that none are Googleable.


2. Sort them by who they're best aimed at: engineer, manager, or skip-level. Do you have at least one strong question for each?


3. Draft one question that demonstrates you've thought about the *challenges* of the role, not just its perks.


4. Practice the habit of noting a follow-up question mid-conversation, so you can reference something the interviewer said earlier.
