# Chapter 10: The mock and the ritual — Try it

*Answers for the Try it questions in [behavioural-chapter-10.md](behavioural-chapter-10.md).*

1. Run a full solo mock: have something generate ten random behavioural questions, and for each, decode the signal and name which bank story you'd play — in under five seconds each. Where you hesitate, you have a gap.


2. Record yourself answering three questions end to end. Score each against the six signals as if you were the interviewer. Be harsh.


3. Do one live mock with a partner who commits to asking *follow-ups.* The follow-ups are the point.


4. Build your night-before checklist now, so it's ready when you actually need it.
