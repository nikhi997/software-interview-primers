# Chapter 9: Red flags, tells, and delivery — Try it

*Answers for the Try it questions in [behavioural-chapter-9.md](behavioural-chapter-9.md).*

1. Take a story involving someone difficult and rewrite the setup to be factual and neutral — zero badmouthing — pivoting quickly to your actions. Notice how much stronger it sounds.


2. Record yourself telling one story. Listen for: rambling, a buried result, hedging language ("kind of," "a little"), or a robotic recited tone. Fix the worst one.


3. Have someone fire a "why did you do it that way?" follow-up at you. Did you stay open and curious, or get defensive? Practice treating the dig as friendly.


4. Audit one story for the confidence/arrogance balance: does it claim your real contribution clearly *and* credit others genuinely?
