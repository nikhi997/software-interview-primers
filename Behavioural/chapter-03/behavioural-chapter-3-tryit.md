# Chapter 3: Building your story bank — Try it

*Answers for the Try it questions in [behavioural-chapter-3.md](behavioural-chapter-3.md).*

1. Set a 30-minute timer and write *raw* one-line notes for as many of the twelve mining prompts above as you can. Don't polish — just capture the memory.


2. Pick your single best project and list *every* signal-bearing moment in it (initiative, conflict, mistake, impact...). Aim for at least three — this is an anchor-story candidate.


3. Fill out the full story-bank template (appendix) for your two strongest stories, including the "what would you do differently?" field.


4. Draw the coverage matrix for the stories you have so far. Which signal column is emptiest? That's your next story to build.
